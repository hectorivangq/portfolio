The project

PLATINUM SHINE MOBILE DETAILING

The job
His prices were on a flyer, and his best proof, his own before and after photos, sat in his camera roll.
What I built
A site that opens on his own work: a before and after slider made from his photos, with his prices right under it.
What changed
Customers see the work and the price before they scroll, and one tap on Book opens a text to Terry, already written. Live since October 8, 2026.
