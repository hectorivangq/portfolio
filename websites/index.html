Questions

BEFORE YOU BOOK.

What if I don't have a logo or photos?

We can start without them. I'll plan photo slots you can fill later, or build your wordmark as part of a brand package.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is yours and the site lives on it.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Yes. Website care can be cancelled any time after the first 3 months, and content packages after 6. I'll hand over everything you need to keep the site running.

Why is the founding rate lower?

I'm building my list of paying clients. You get a lower price, I get a case study I can show.

How fast can you build a website?

Five business days from kickoff. Your photos and info are due by day 2, so I can build without waiting. If yours needs longer, I'll tell you on the first call.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Keeping the site online on your domain, updates, up to 2 change requests a month (like a new photo or new hours) and a monthly look at your analytics. Bigger changes get a separate quote. Hosting costs you nothing extra.
