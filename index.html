Questions

BEFORE YOU BOOK.

How fast can you build a website?

Five business days from kickoff. Your photos and info are due by day 2, so I can build without waiting. If yours needs longer, I'll tell you on the first call.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Keeping the site online on your domain, updates, up to 2 change requests a month (like a new photo or new hours) and a monthly look at your analytics. Bigger changes get a separate quote. Hosting costs you nothing extra.

Do you only work in Texas?

No. I work remotely anywhere in the US and Puerto Rico. Filming happens in person around Dallas.

¿Hablas español?

Sí. I work in English and Spanish, and I can build your site in both.

What do I need to have ready?

Your logo if you have one, photos of your work and 30 minutes. I write the words with you.

Do you guarantee rankings or leads?

No. Nobody honest can. I guarantee the build and the monthly work, because those I control.
