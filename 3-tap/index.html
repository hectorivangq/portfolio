The test

THREE TAPS. YOUR PHONE. YOUR WEBSITE.

Open your website on your phone, at the top of the page. Don't scroll yet. Try each tap the way a new customer would.

  1. 1

    Tap 1 CALL

    From the top of the page, can someone call you with one tap?

    Passing looks like

    • A Call button they can see without scrolling
    • One tap opens the call screen with your number already in it
    • It stays in reach as they scroll, at the top or in a bar at the bottom

    Failing looks like

    • Your number is plain text or part of a picture, so they have to copy it
    • The only way to reach you is a contact form
    • They have to open a menu or scroll to the footer to find it

    On a site I built

    On Platinum Shine, Call sits in a bar at the bottom of every screen, next to Text and Book a detail. There's a call button at the top too.

  2. 2

    Tap 2 PRICE AND AREA

    Can they see what it costs and where you work, without scrolling?

    Passing looks like

    • A starting price on the first screen, even if it says “and up”
    • The towns or area you serve, in plain words
    • Both easy to read without zooming in

    Failing looks like

    • “Call for a quote” and no number anywhere
    • The area you serve shows up only in the footer, or nowhere
    • Prices live in a PDF or a photo of a flyer

    On a site I built

    Platinum Shine shows “$75 and up” and “$120 sedan” on the first screen, and “Springfield, MA” sits right above the headline.

  3. 3

    Tap 3 BOOK

    Can they book in one step?

    Passing looks like

    • One tap starts the booking: a text that's already written, a call, or a page with open times
    • The button says what it does, like “Book a detail”, not “Submit”
    • They can book without making an account

    Failing looks like

    • A long form, then “We'll get back to you”
    • A “Contact us” page with no clear next step
    • A booking link that's broken or opens a blank page

    On a site I built

    On Platinum Shine, the Text button opens your messages with this already written:

    Hi Terry, I'd like to book a detail. When are you available?
