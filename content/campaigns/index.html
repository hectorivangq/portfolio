CAMPAIGNS

Campaign concepts

SIX BRANDS. SIX FEEDS.

Concept campaigns I planned and designed for made-up businesses, so you can see how I'd build a feed for yours. Each one starts from a goal, not a template.

Every brand on this page is fictional. These are concepts, not client work.

The method

HOW I BUILD A CAMPAIGN.

01

Goal

One business goal per campaign: regulars, sign-ups, sales or booked calls. Every post has to serve it.

02

Pillars

Three content pillars, so the feed has range without wandering off topic.

03

Look

A visual system of two typefaces, a short palette and repeatable layouts, so every post is recognizably the brand.

04

Measure

One number to watch per goal (saves, link clicks, messages or sign-ups), reviewed every month.

CORNER POUR

Neighborhood café · InstagramConcept · fictional brand

A warm, editorial feed that sells the feeling of having a usual table, not just the coffee.

Goal
Turn local followers into weekday regulars.
Audience
Remote workers, students and commuters who live or work within ten minutes.
Pillars
The menu up close · The people behind the bar · Reasons to stay a while
Formats
Type-led posts, latte-art Reels, menu carousels
Cadence
4 feed posts a week, plus daily Stories
Look
Warm photography in espresso and cream, cream and pistachio type tiles, heavy lowercase with one italic word, an order ticket.

Community run club · Event campaignConcept · fictional brand

A free 5K promoted like a gig poster, then turned into a weekly club.
